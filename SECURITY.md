# Security Policy

## Our Commitment

At upTools, security is a top priority. We are committed to ensuring the security and privacy of our users and their data. We appreciate the community's efforts in helping us maintain high security standards through responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

### How to Report

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in any upTools-owned repository or service, please report it to us through our dedicated security email:

**Email:** security@uptools.io

### What to Include

When reporting a vulnerability, please include:

1. Description of the vulnerability
2. Steps to reproduce the issue
3. Potential impact of the vulnerability
4. Any possible mitigations
5. System/platform details where the vulnerability was discovered

### What to Expect

After you submit a vulnerability report:

1. **Acknowledgment**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 24 hours.
2. **Assessment**: Our security team will investigate and validate the issue.
3. **Updates**: We will keep you informed about our progress.
4. **Resolution**: Once fixed, we will notify you and discuss public disclosure if appropriate.

## Response Timeline

- **24 hours**: Initial acknowledgment of report
- **72 hours**: Initial assessment completed
- **7 days**: Regular updates on progress
- **90 days**: Standard timeline for vulnerability resolution

## Scope

This security policy applies to all repositories and services owned and maintained by upTools.

### In Scope

- All public repositories under the upTools organization
- Our production services and infrastructure
- Official releases and packages

### Out of Scope

- Third-party applications or services
- Vulnerabilities already reported
- Social engineering attacks
- DOS/DDOS attacks

## Best Practices

We recommend following these security best practices when using upTools products:

1. Keep all dependencies up to date
2. Use strong authentication methods
3. Follow the principle of least privilege
4. Regularly audit access and permissions
5. Enable security features and logging

## Disclosure Policy

- We follow responsible disclosure practices
- Public disclosure is coordinated with reporters
- Credit is given to reporters who follow this policy
- We aim to fix vulnerabilities before public disclosure

## Security Updates

We regularly publish security updates through:

- Security advisories on GitHub
- Our official blog
- Security mailing list

To stay informed, we recommend:
- Watching our repositories
- Following our security announcements
- Subscribing to our security mailing list

## Thank You

We value the security research community and believe in the positive impact of coordinated vulnerability disclosure. Thank you for helping us maintain a secure environment for all our users.
